Chess Coach 1.1.0 refreshes the board screens and adds more useful coaching controls, with fixes for progress saving, sync and game review.

## What's new
- Rounded interface, a new knight app icon, centered phone boards and improved Puzzles/Lessons layouts.
- A prominent Hint button, Redo, and a compact Game options menu.
- Android PiP coach actions: tap the floating window to reveal Undo, Hint and Play hint. Request Hint twice to reveal a move, then use Play hint to play it. Expand the window to move pieces directly.
- Short split-screen layouts keep guidance and controls accessible. Resizing preserves the active game.
- Light, Dark and Wooden remain available, alongside Midnight, Amethyst and Sand. Choose Glass, Fluent or Material styling in Settings.

## Fixes
- Hints cancel safely when the position changes; offline hints now highlight a piece and reveal a legal suggestion.
- Fixed captured-piece player-bar overflow, short learning panels, lesson demo replay and puzzle reply/solution races.
- Improved game-review error recovery, saved training-session progress and daily progress refresh.
- Serialized local history writes and recovery from truncated or damaged records.
- Fixed sync changes arriving during uploads, settings-only sync, sign-out races and account-token isolation.
- Google Drive merges snapshots from each device instead of allowing competing uploads to overwrite one shared file.
- Update installation status now waits for Android's completion result; update/review prompts defer while in PiP.

## Updating
Install over the previous release to keep your data. Existing installations discover this release through the in-app updater; Android may ask you to approve installation.

For two-way Google Drive sync, update **all** your devices. This version imports older sync history, but older versions cannot read the new snapshot files. Sync remains optional and requires Google sign-in.

Android requires version 7 or newer; native PiP requires Android 8 or newer and device support. The Android download is for arm64 devices. This release does not claim universal device coverage or zero remaining bugs.
