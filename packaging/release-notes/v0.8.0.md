A friendlier coach, sync between your devices, and lots of polish.

## Friendly explanations (optional)
- Settings → Coach voice: download a small language model (491 MB, or 1.1 GB for nicer English on a
  computer). It runs on your device, offline, and rewords the coach's explanations in plain English.
- The chess facts still come from Stockfish. Any answer that adds or drops a move, square, piece or threat
  is thrown away, and the coach uses its standard sentence. Without the model everything works as before.

## Google Drive sync
- Keeps your phone, Windows and Ubuntu in step through one small file in a hidden app folder of your
  Google Drive. Games, puzzles, lessons and reviews from every device are merged; nothing is overwritten.
- It syncs when the app opens and when you close it, and retries a few times if you're offline: no battery
  drain. Sync appears in Settings once the Google sign-in is set up (see `docs/google-drive-sync.md`).

## Polish
- Smoother progress bars and coach messages; the system "reduce motion" setting is respected.
- Works with large system fonts on phones; boards and buttons are labelled for screen readers.
- Keyboard: arrow keys and Enter move through lessons and review cards.
- Clear messages when something can't load or save, instead of a blank screen.
- Faster on phones with a long history.

## Install
- **Android:** open `ChessCoach-0.8.0-android-arm64.apk` and allow installing from this source. It installs
  over earlier versions and keeps your data.
- **Windows:** run `ChessCoach-0.8.0-Setup.exe`. If SmartScreen warns about an unknown publisher,
  choose *More info → Run anyway*. A portable `.zip` is attached too.
- **Ubuntu:** `chmod +x ChessCoach-0.8.0-x86_64.AppImage && ./ChessCoach-0.8.0-x86_64.AppImage`
