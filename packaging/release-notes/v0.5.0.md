Lessons, puzzles and a coach that plans your training.

## Lessons (new)
- 20 interactive lessons from beginner to 2000: your opening repertoire (Italian Game as White, 1…e5 and
  the Queen's Gambit Declined as Black), tactics, pawn structure, planning, and the essential endgames
  (basic mates, opposition, the square of the pawn, Lucena and Philidor, bishop versus knight, converting).
- Each lesson mixes short explanations, moves played on the board, and "find the move" exercises checked
  like puzzles, with hints and answers. Every exercise was checked with Stockfish.
- Your progress is saved: the Lessons screen shows what is done, and Home offers the lesson to continue.

## Puzzles
- 30,000 Lichess puzzles (400–2400) with a puzzle rating, theme training and puzzles made from your own
  mistakes.

## Your personal coach
- **"How much time do you have?"** now builds a real session (2 minutes to 2 hours): warm-up, lesson,
  review, themed puzzles, endgame drill or a training game. The coach learns which kinds of training help
  you most and adjusts the plan.
- **Skill ratings** for tactics, openings, middlegame, endgames and blunder checking, from your games,
  puzzles and lessons. Home shows your estimated rating, training streak and skills (no more preview data).
- **Spaced repetition review:** repertoire moves, mistakes from your games and missed puzzles come back
  just before you would forget them.
- **Coach strength follows you:** by default the coach plays a little above your level, so you win a bit
  less than half your games. You can still pick a fixed strength.

## Install
- **Android:** open `ChessCoach-0.5.0-android-arm64.apk` and allow installing from this source. It
  installs over 0.2.0 and keeps your data.
- **Windows:** run `ChessCoach-0.5.0-Setup.exe`. If SmartScreen warns about an unknown publisher,
  choose *More info → Run anyway*. A portable `.zip` is attached too.
- **Ubuntu:** `chmod +x ChessCoach-0.5.0-x86_64.AppImage && ./ChessCoach-0.5.0-x86_64.AppImage`
