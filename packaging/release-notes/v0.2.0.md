Your chess coach for Android, Windows and Ubuntu.

## Play
- The coach plays **like a human** from about 400 to 1900 (Maia neural networks trained on real games),
  and uses Stockfish 19 from 2000 up to full strength.
- Play White, Black or a random colour; undo, resign; your unfinished game is restored when you reopen the app.

## Learn while you play (Training mode)
- Every move graded: best, good, inaccuracy, mistake, blunder.
- Short reasons from the engine ("Now Black can take your knight on f6") and the better move on the board.
- Two-step hints (first which piece, then the move) and an evaluation bar.
- Real game mode keeps the coach quiet until the end.

## Review every game
- Accuracy for you and the coach, your key mistakes, the better move with arrows, step through every move
  (arrow keys work on desktop). Recent games are on the Home screen.

## Install
- **Android:** open `ChessCoach-0.2.0-android-arm64.apk` and allow installing from this source.
  Future updates install over this version and keep your data.
- **Windows:** run `ChessCoach-0.2.0-Setup.exe`. If SmartScreen warns about an unknown publisher,
  choose *More info → Run anyway*. A portable `.zip` is attached too.
- **Ubuntu:** `chmod +x ChessCoach-0.2.0-x86_64.AppImage && ./ChessCoach-0.2.0-x86_64.AppImage`
