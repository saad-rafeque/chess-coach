Chess Coach

A free, open-source chess coach for Android, Windows and Ubuntu. It plays you at your level like a human would, explains your mistakes in plain English, and builds a training plan that adapts to you.

Download Source code Privacy policy

What it does

  • A coach that plays like a person at any strength from 400 to 3200, and can pick the right strength for you.
  • Training mode comments on every move, gives hints and shows the better move when you slip.
  • Game review with accuracy, key moments and arrows for the best moves.
  • 30,000 puzzles rated to your level, plus puzzles made from your own mistakes.
  • 20 lessons on openings, middlegames and endgames, with exercises.
  • A personal plan: skill ratings, spaced-repetition review cards and short training sessions.
  • Friendly explanations from an optional small language model that runs on your device.

Your data stays yours

Everything is stored on your device. There is no account, no server, no ads and no tracking.

If you want your progress on several devices, connect your own Google account. Chess Coach then keeps one small file in a hidden app folder of your Google Drive. It can't see any of your other files, and nobody else, including the developer, can see your data. Read the privacy policy.

Install

  • Android: download the .apk, open it and allow installing from this source.
  • Windows: run the Setup.exe. On a SmartScreen warning choose More info → Run anyway.
  • Ubuntu: sudo apt install ./ChessCoach-<version>-amd64.deb, or use the AppImage.
