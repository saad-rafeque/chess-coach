Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Chess Coach is a free, open-source chess training app for Android, Windows and Linux, made by Saad Rafique. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your data stays on your devices and, if you choose, in your own Google Drive. The developer never receives it.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your games, game reviews, puzzle attempts, lesson progress, review cards and settings on your device: in the Documents/Chess Coach folder on Windows and Linux, and in the app's private storage on Android. It is used only to run the app: to show your history, rate your skills and plan your training.

There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not send this data anywhere unless you turn on Google Drive sync.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you choose Connect in Settings, you sign in with Google in your browser and allow one permission: "See, create and delete its own configuration data in your Google Drive" (drive.appdata). With it, Chess Coach:

  • keeps one file, chess-coach-sync.json.gz, with your app data and settings, in a hidden app folder of your Google Drive, so your devices stay in step;
  • cannot see, open or change any other file in your Google Drive, and does not access your email, contacts or any other Google data;
  • sends this file only between your device and Google. There is no Chess Coach server, and the developer never receives, stores, shares or sells your data;
  • stores your Google sign-in only on your device.

Chess Coach's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The data is used only to provide sync to you; it is never used for advertising, never transferred to others, and never read by people.

Stopping sync and deleting your data

  • Disconnect in Settings removes the sign-in from that device and revokes the app's access.
  • You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • To delete the synced file, open Google Drive on the web, go to Settings → Manage apps, find Chess Coach and choose Delete hidden app data.
  • Uninstalling the app on Android deletes its data on the phone. On Windows and Linux, delete the Documents/Chess Coach folder.

Optional language model download

If you choose to download the optional language model in Settings, the app downloads a file from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). Like any download, Hugging Face sees your IP address. The model then runs entirely on your device; nothing you do in the app is sent to it or to anyone else.

Children

Chess Coach collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. The app's source code is public at github.com/saad-rafeque/chess-coach, so anyone can check what it does.
